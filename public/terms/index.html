Scrobbler for Sonos

Terms

Short, because the service is small: it reads what your Sonos plays and forwards it to the scrobbling accounts you connected.

What you are agreeing to

  • You are connecting accounts you control — your Sonos household, your Last.fm account, your ListenBrainz token.
  • You may stop at any time by signing out, pressing Delete everything, or revoking the integration from your Sonos account.
  • You will not use the service to submit plays you did not listen to. Last.fm and ListenBrainz both prohibit that, and it is their accounts at risk, not ours.

What is not promised

The service is provided as is, with no warranty and no uptime guarantee. It depends on three external services — Sonos's event delivery, Last.fm's API and ListenBrainz's API — any of which can change or stop. Sonos in particular discards an event it cannot deliver, so a play that happens during an outage is not recoverable.

A play that is submitted and rejected by a scrobbling service stays rejected; this service reports the reason on the status page rather than retrying forever.
