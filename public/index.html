Your way back in

Sign-in link There is no password here. Signing in with Sonos is the account. If you clear your cookies, switch browser, or leave it a month, this link is how you get back to these settings. Save it in your notes, your bookmarks or your password manager. Anyone who has it can sign in as you.

Lost it, or shared it by mistake? The old one stops working straight away.

We keep no listening history. A track exists here only while it plays. After that it lives on Last.fm and ListenBrainz, which is where you read it back.
